# Kevin Vault (starter)

A starting structure for your Obsidian knowledge base. Copy this folder to your
vault location and open it in Obsidian. See
[../README.md](../README.md) for plugin + Ollama setup.

## Structure

```
00 Inbox/                 quick capture; sort later
01 A Better You/          SOPs, pricing, policies, FBX programs, marketing
02 Business Acquisitions/ clinics, gyms, CIM summaries, due diligence, broker emails
03 Financing/             ITIN-friendly lenders, BDC, CSBFP, bank requirements
04 Contracts/             NDAs, agreements, templates
05 Content/               Carb Conspiracy, YouTube scripts, sales pages
06 Personal/              goals, preferences, notes
07 Research Sources/      PDFs->Markdown (from scripts/ingest), web clips, transcripts
08 AI Outputs/            summaries, flashcards, podcasts, reports
_templates/               Templater templates
```

## Tips
- Keep notes small and linked — RAG retrieves better from focused notes than
  giant dumps.
- Run **Smart Connections** to surface related notes as you write.
- Use **Copilot → Vault QA** to ask questions across everything with citations.
- For deep Q&A or an audio overview on a topic, export the relevant note as a
  source into an Open Notebook notebook.
